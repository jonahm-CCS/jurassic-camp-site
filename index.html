Made for James

Built with heart.
Inspired by imagination.

Jurassic Camp was created by a father for his son, James — a lifelong dinosaur lover, designer and maker. From sculpting dinosaurs out of aluminum foil as a child to imagining sophisticated animatronics, his creativity inspired the world of this game.

In the story, James is a young mechanical engineer whose robotic dinosaurs were once the pride of a thriving park. A cyberattack has turned his creations into its greatest threat.

“For the dinosaur lovers, builders, dreamers and creators who never stopped imagining.”— A dedication to James
