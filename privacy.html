Privacy Policy

Jurassic Camp: Survival
Effective date: October 27, 2026
Developer: Jonah Mericle

Important: This policy is a pre-release draft. The final game build and any analytics, crash-reporting, music, advertising or other SDK integrations must be checked before this policy is used for an App Store submission.

1. Overview

Jurassic Camp: Survival is an independently developed dinosaur-themed strategy game. We aim to minimize the personal information required to play and to explain transparently how information is handled.

2. Information you provide

The game is intended to allow gameplay without requiring players to provide their name, email address, or phone number or register an account. If you email our support address, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your email address and message, to respond to your request.

3. Game progress and preferences

Game progress, settings and preferences may be stored on your device to support gameplay. The exact storage behavior of the released version will be reviewed before publication.

4. Technical information and third parties

Device diagnostics, analytics, crash reports, and information processed by external integrations depend on the features and software development kits included in the released application. We will update this section to identify applicable providers, purposes and data categories after the release build is verified.

5. Advertising and tracking

Any advertising or cross-app tracking practices, if implemented, will be described here and reflected in the relevant App Store disclosures and permission requests.

6. Children's privacy

We do not intend to solicit children's personal information through regular gameplay. Parents or guardians with concerns may contact us using the details below. Any applicable child-privacy practices will be reviewed against the released app's features.

7. Security and retention

We seek to minimize the collection of personal data and take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to requests and manage support communications.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The current version and its effective date will be available on this page.

9. Contact

For questions, requests or privacy concerns, contact Jonah Mericle at jonah.mericle@gmail.com.
