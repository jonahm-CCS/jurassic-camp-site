Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Build and upgrade defenses, protect your survivors from attacking dinosaurs, and survive 30 waves to reach helicopter extraction.

How do I upgrade or sell a turret?

Select a placed turret to access its available management actions. Available options depend on the current game phase and the installed app version.

How do reprogramming traps work?

Reprogramming traps can turn opposing dinosaurs into allies to help defend your camp.

How do I report a bug?

Email us a short description of the problem, the steps that caused it, your device model and iOS version. Include a screenshot if possible, but do not send passwords or sensitive information.

How can I suggest improvements?

We'd love your feedback! Send your gameplay ideas or accessibility suggestions to the support email above.
